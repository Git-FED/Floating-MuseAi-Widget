The helpful kind of interruption

Less app-hopping. More getting things done.

Floating Muse is designed for quick questions, quick replies, and quick returns. Drag the button where you like it. The overlay avoids the status bar, navigation bar, lock screen, and permission dialogs.

A small footprint

It does not need to be dramatic.

The helper service uses a foreground notification and asks only for the permissions it needs: overlay, internet, boot restore, foreground service, and notifications on newer Android versions.
